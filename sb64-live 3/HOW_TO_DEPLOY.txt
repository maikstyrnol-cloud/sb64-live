HOW TO DEPLOY YOUR LIVE SB64 DASHBOARD
=======================================
Total time: about 10 minutes. No coding needed.

WHAT YOU HAVE
-------------
This folder contains 3 files:
  - index.html        (the dashboard page)
  - api/schedule.js   (the live fetch engine)
  - vercel.json       (tells Vercel how to run it)

STEP 1 — Go to Vercel
-----------------------
Open your browser and go to:
  https://vercel.com

Click "Sign Up" → choose "Continue with Email"
Enter your email, verify it, done.

STEP 2 — Deploy by drag and drop
----------------------------------
1. After signing up, you'll see a dashboard.
2. Click "Add New" → "Project"
3. On the next screen, look for "Or deploy from your local files" 
   and click "Browse" or drag the entire "sb64-live" folder 
   onto the upload area.
   
   (If you don't see drag-and-drop, click "Import" and select the folder)

4. Leave all settings as they are — don't change anything.
5. Click "Deploy"
6. Wait about 30 seconds.
7. Vercel gives you a URL like: https://sb64-live-abc123.vercel.app

STEP 3 — Open your live dashboard
------------------------------------
Click the URL Vercel gives you. 
Bookmark it. That's your live dashboard.

It will:
- Auto-refresh every 3 minutes
- Pull new side events from the UNFCCC SEORS database
- Highlight forest-relevant new additions in green
- Show you the current day automatically

STEP 4 — Optional: give it a nicer URL
-----------------------------------------
In Vercel → your project → Settings → Domains
You can add a free custom subdomain like:
  sb64-forest.vercel.app
(just pick any name you like, it's free)

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- "Fetch failed" in the status bar: SEORS is temporarily unreachable. 
  The pre-loaded data still works. It will auto-retry in 3 minutes.

- Page looks broken: Try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R)

- Want to re-deploy with updates: Just drag the folder again in Vercel.

WHAT UPDATES LIVE
-----------------
✓ New side events added to SEORS (the official UNFCCC side events database)
✓ Forest-relevant ones highlighted automatically
✓ New-event badges (🆕) appear on the relevant day

WHAT DOES NOT UPDATE LIVE
--------------------------
✗ The negotiations schedule (it's JavaScript-rendered by UNFCCC,
  no public data feed exists). Use the "Negotiations Tracker" tab
  which links you directly to the live UNFCCC page for each day.

QUESTIONS?
----------
Ask Claude to update the HTML with new events or fix anything.
Just paste the updated files into Vercel again.
